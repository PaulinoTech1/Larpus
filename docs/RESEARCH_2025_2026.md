## Why fresh information is necessary

The local model's training knowledge is a fixed baseline. Downloaded, vetted
references close later knowledge gaps without retraining, but only after admission
and retrieval integration. A download or prepared study-note queue alone does not
make its contents available to the live assistant. Refreshes should follow relevant
changes, corrections and user needs rather than indiscriminately replacing older
work with newer papers.

Personas share the reference collection but differ in sensitivity to stale evidence:

| Personas | Freshness sensitivity and refresh triggers |
| --- | --- |
| Home Assistant | Very high for AI products, model availability, plan limits and API pricing. Verify current official documentation before recommendations; retain the date and currency of prices. |
| IT/cyber coach; System Administrator; Linux Kernel Expert; Network Engineer | High for vulnerabilities, support lifecycles, kernel behavior, cloud/identity configuration and vendor defaults. Match the exact product, release and environment; older instructions can fail or weaken security. |
| Cryptographer | High for standards, algorithm status, post-quantum migration and implementation guidance. Foundational proofs remain useful, but current recommendations require edition and errata checks. |
| Economist; Sociologist; Psychologist | High for current measurements, populations, policy and intervention evidence. Retain observation periods, study scope, revisions and corrections; a recent publication can still analyze older data. |
| Quantum Mechanics Professor | High for experimental capabilities and hardware claims; lower for established theory. Distinguish demonstrated results from proposals and preserve noise/code assumptions. |
| Statistician; Mathematician | Lower for established definitions and proven results, higher for corrected proofs, method assumptions, software and emerging research. Publication age alone does not invalidate a theorem or guarantee a newer method is better. |

These are curation priorities, not an implemented automatic expiry system. Record
publication, revision, observation/effective and review dates separately. Establish
supersession or correction only with scoped publisher evidence. Keep historical
sources available with their status, preserve authors independently of bibliography
names, and identify missing current evidence instead of presenting stale material as
current. Research novelty and operational currency are separate questions.
