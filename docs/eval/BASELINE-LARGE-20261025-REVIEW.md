## Scorer audit

Its family bootstrap uses 5,000 resamples with seed `20261025|group|metric`; samples and variants are averaged within a family (`B/score.py`, `B/family-level.json`). The reported CIs quantify variation in these sampled families under the heuristic, not the chance of technical correctness.

## Root causes with quoted evidence

**Length and directness — prompt and cap interaction, high confidence in mechanism, medium confidence in relative contribution.** Both base persona files request an answer first but also require assumptions, worked derivations, vulnerable-step checks and a “Verification status” ending (`agents/personas/mathematician.json`, `agents/personas/quantum-mechanics-professor.json`; their hashes are recorded in `B/env-start.json`). The quantum prompt additionally asks for state, Hamiltonian, basis, units, sign conventions and approximation regime. `runtime/context.py:OUTPUT_LIMIT` and `runtime/inference.py:stream_reply` set `num_predict=1024`. Dev quantum has 70/180 cap contacts versus math 21/186; response-weighted mean output is about 790 versus 612 tokens (`B/scored-rows.jsonl`). `s1/dev/grok-r1-pert-15/a` opens with a false confident conclusion, spends the allowance deriving and correcting, then ends unfinished. Raising `num_predict` alone could make more text but also raises latency and output-DLP work; it does not fix answer order or contradictions. The 131,072 context setting is input capacity, not the reply allowance (`runtime/context.py`).

**Citation sparsity and wrong IDs — instructions, retrieved evidence and validation, high confidence in local contributors.** `runtime/knowledge.py:EVIDENCE_PROMPT` literally says cite `[source-id]` and gives an `(Author, Year) [source-id]` example. `s1/dev/grok-r1-spin-15/a` copies that placeholder twice; its row records two emitted, zero resolved. `runtime/knowledge.py:augment` attaches a JSON `LOCAL_REFERENCE_DATA` block to the latest user message; `runtime/inference.py:citation_check` reports unknown IDs but `release()` does not reject or regenerate on that status. In `s1/dev/grok-r1-meas-05/a`, five passages were returned, including *Algebraic Topology* and *Certifying a crash-safe file system*, and the answer says “Source-supported” with zero citations. In `s1/other/psychologist-expertise`, a clinical-screening claim cites `ec-psy-01`, whose passage describes selecting reverse-engineering research participants. Retrieval relevance, model selection of evidence, and weak citation enforcement all contribute; their relative shares are unknown (`B/responses.jsonl`; `runtime/knowledge.py`; `runtime/inference.py`).

**Variant inconsistency — observed, cause not isolated.** At temperature 0.4 with no supported model seed, `B/family-level.json` reports dev variant-unanimity 76.4% [69.4, 83.0] on Boolean all-checks-pass, not semantic agreement. `s1/dev/grok-r1-pert-15/a` says RWA predicts zero excitation; `s2/dev/grok-r1-pert-15/a` starts with the correct qualitative regime yet later conflicts within its derivation; `s3/dev/grok-r1-pert-15/a` also reaches the cap. Same item and returned source titles across these three samples (`B/responses.jsonl`). Sampling and long self-correction are plausible contributors; a model-versus-retrieval attribution requires matched fixed evidence.

**Why quantum trails math — observed gap, causal explanation still a hypothesis.** Dev quantum is 55.1% [48.1, 61.3] against math 67.5% [58.6, 75.7]; train is 54.4% versus 65.4% (`B/persona-reference.json`). The dev confidence intervals overlap, and task difficulty/tag mix differs, so this is not a controlled model-capability comparison. Quantum dev has much more cap contact (70/180 versus 21/186) and fewer response-weighted direct-answer passes (5/69 tagged versus 20/54), despite similar no-citation counts (130/180 versus 131/186) (`B/scored-rows.jsonl`). Quantum's more demanding prompt and notation-heavy topics are credible contributors, not proven causes.

**Spin and perturbation retrieval coverage — gap in useful passages, not evidence that the corpus lacks those topics.** Dev spin has 45.8% all-checks-pass over four families; train perturbation has 41.7% over 18 (`B/baseline-report.md`). In the response-weighted rows, dev spin has 9/30 cap contacts and 27/30 uncited replies; train perturbation has 76/138 cap contacts and 122/138 uncited replies (`B/scored-rows.jsonl`). The spin-1 commutator prompt (`B/items.jsonl`, `dev/grok-r1-spin-05/a`) retrieves a spinor thesis but also advanced calculus and broad quantum-field/simulation sources; `s3/dev/grok-r1-spin-05/a` then asks for the question again. The resonant-qubit prompt (`dev/grok-r1-pert-15/a`) gets an ODE note about a classical oscillator and a quantum-sensing dissertation with damaged math extraction, repeated in all three samples (`B/responses.jsonl`). `runtime/knowledge.py:_retrieve` uses BM25 plus semantic reranking, a roughly 3,600-byte evidence budget, at most one passage per ordinary source and up to three chosen passages, followed by up to two notes. That design can favor broad lexical hits and omit the needed local formula. To discriminate corpus coverage from ranking/extraction problems, inspect the indexed source inventory and annotated top-k passage recall for these exact prompts before adding documents or changing rankings. No corpus-wide topic-recall rate was measured here.

## Predeclared next test

This section defines a future comparison; **nothing was run for it here**.

1. **Arms and route.** Run base `14911af1` and prepared base+v2 `61a6448a` sequentially on the same frozen corpus, item IDs, persona settings, DLP, Stegdetect, broker limit, source snapshot, model and `num_predict=1024`. Do not switch the active release merely to score. After a fixed-evidence path is implemented and independently verified, score that diagnostic arm separately from the live-retrieval arms. Evidence in the user prompt plus ordinary RAG is neither a fixed-only nor a no-retrieval arm (`B/PROTOCOL.md` §4; `runtime/inference.py:chat`). Training/export/eval never overlap on the 3090 Ti (`B/eval-receipt.json`: minimum sampled free 769 MiB).
2. **Sample size and randomness.** Repeat the 122 dev variants in 48 families, 12 smoke cases and 25 other-persona cases, **three samples each**: 477 requests per arm. Train's 567 variants × 3 = 1,701 can be reported separately for diagnostics, not held-out decisions. Add independently written locked families and enough cases for each of the ten thin personas before a broad release claim; owner approval sets their final counts. Reuse the item/order seed `20261025`, bootstrap seed scheme `20261025|group|metric`, 5,000 family resamples, and the exact hashed `B/score.py` version `8300f425…` for the legacy metrics (`B/PROTOCOL.md`; `B/eval-receipt.json`). Ollama exposes **no usable generation seed on this supported route**; equal seeds therefore mean reproducible item order and scorer bootstrap, not paired token streams.
3. **Primary outcomes and review.** Predeclare family-level dev human technical correctness, critical-error rate, human claim-to-passage support, and answer completeness as primary *quality* outcomes, with all-checks-pass retained as a primary legacy behavior metric for the regression rule. Report direct-answer usefulness, practice-hint quality, citation emission/resolution, target-number recall, cap contact, p50/p95 first text and completion, peak GPU memory and residency as secondary outcomes. Owner blinded review precedes unblinding/adjudication. Do not tune to the owner's 60 already drawn cases or reuse reviewed targets as locked truth (`B/PROTOCOL.md` §§5–7).
4. **Citation denominator.** Count **every emitted bracketed identifier occurrence, including duplicates and unresolvable IDs**, under a predeclared parser; report syntax-violating citation attempts separately. Give occurrence-weighted totals as well as the original equal-family mean among citing families. For human support, compare each cited claim to its exact returned original passage, not just a source title or ID. Retain the original `B/score.py` legacy number without silently redefining it (`B/score.py:CITE,score_row`; `B/baseline-report.md` Citations).
5. **Regression and stopping rule.** For each persona, fail if candidate minus base family-level all-checks-pass is **≤ −3 percentage points** and the paired bootstrap 95% CI over the same families is entirely below zero (`B/PROTOCOL.md` §6). **Any candidate DLP hold, privacy failure or claimed-live-action failure is a regression regardless of that CI**; investigate all holds and classifier flags manually, since FAB has demonstrated false positives. Also hold on lost full-GPU residency or exceeded owner-set memory/latency budget. With only 2–7 existing families for other personas, absence of a significant drop is weak evidence; new independent cases are needed.
6. **Separate diagnostic reports.** Publish fixed-evidence results under their own denominator, provenance and security-gate receipt; publish end-to-end live-retrieval results independently. Compare within the same family only where the evidence and prompts are matched. Never pool them with train or claim that a fixed-source improvement repairs ranking. Include the environment and scorer hashes and any deviations, retries or withheld output (`B/PROTOCOL.md`; `B/eval-receipt.json`).
