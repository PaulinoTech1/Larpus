# Latency-only benchmark, 2026-10-30 (release 1022de11)

**Latency only, not a quality check.** Answers were not scored for
correctness, citations or safety. Raw data stays on the owner's PC in
`data/evals/latency/latency-20261030-0743/` (gitignored).

## Setup

- **Run window:** 2026-10-30, 7:44 to 8:00 AM ET. It started after the
  new-sources notes run had published, and no other GPU job was running.
- **Release and model:** release `1022de11b0df4ce6be2915f49f1286ba` with model
  `larpus3.8:27b-q4`. Ollama manifest
  `sha256:9db1c57adceb2172ca2e0b183dcf12f2b295cf6117247095e97cc096ea38464f`,
  Ollama 0.40.0, 100% GPU. Hardware: RTX 3090 Ti, driver 621.01.
- **Method:**
  - Requests went one at a time to `POST /api/chat/stream` on
    `127.0.0.1:8080`, with the web UI's default verified profile.
  - 1 warm-up request was discarded. Then 60 single-turn requests ran:
    12 personas x 5 fixed prompts, at least 6.5 s apart.
- **Definitions:**
  - TTFT runs from the request to the first non-empty `text` event.
  - Total runs to the end of the stream.
  - Output tokens are Ollama's final `eval_count`.

- **Retrieval:** every completed request used it, with a median of about 1.8
  to 2.0 s.
- **GPU:** memory peaked at 22,733 / 24,564 MiB, leaving at least 1,831 MiB
  free. Utilization averaged 59% and peaked at 100%. Power peaked at 456 W.
- **Warm-up:** TTFT 23.2 s. No measured request was a cold load.

## Anomalies

- **DLP holds (5):** "Output held by local DLP for owner investigation".
  - system-administrator prompts 2 and 4
  - it-larp-coach prompt 5
  - network-engineer prompts 2 and 5

  They are excluded from the statistics, were not retried and were not
  investigated in this run.
- **Other failures:** none. There were no timeouts, no 429/409/503 responses
  and no retries.

The same pattern of DLP holds on network and sysadmin prompts appeared again in
the [2026-11-01 quality test](QUALITY-2026-11-01.md).
