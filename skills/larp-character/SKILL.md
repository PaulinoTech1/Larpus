# Character preparation

Ask for the setting, character goal and desired tone. Build a compact persona
with motivations, social boundaries, vocabulary and three scene hooks.
Separate in-character lines from out-of-character coaching. When the owner wants
practice, play one scene beat at a time and leave room for their response.
Respect the owner's consent boundaries and distinguish fiction from credentials
or authority in the real world.
