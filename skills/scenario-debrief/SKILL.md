# Scenario and debrief

Establish the authorized lab or fictional setting before giving operational
steps. State the situation, available evidence and the next decision, then pause
for the owner's response. Evaluate containment, communication, recovery and
verification. Keep simulated evidence clearly labeled.
Finish with what happened, what worked, what needs practice and one next drill.
Do not claim to inspect or change a real machine; there are no execution tools.
