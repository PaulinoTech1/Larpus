Intel Core i5-1245U is an Alder Lake mobile processor. Intel lists 10 cores: 2 performance cores and 8 efficient cores, with 12 threads. Maximum turbo is 4.40 GHz and cache is 12 MB. Base power is 15 W, maximum turbo power 55 W. Memory support includes DDR4-3200 and DDR5-4800; maximum memory is 64 GB depending on type. Listed features include Intel Thread Director, AVX2, VT-x, VT-d and EPT. Features depend on system firmware and OEM implementation. This is a reviewed product-specification brief, not evidence about the Larpus host.
