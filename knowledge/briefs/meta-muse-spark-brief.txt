Reviewed 2026-10-09. Muse Spark is the Meta model family for agentic work, coding and long-context reasoning; Muse is the personal agent built around models. Meta lists muse-spark-1.3 as latest/recommended, with 1.2 and 1.1 also available. All have 1,048,576-token context and text output, accepting text, images, video, audio and PDF; audio understanding in 1.3 is incomplete, so Meta recommends 1.2 or Muse Voice Transcribe for audio. Spark 1.3 supports max reasoning on Standard only. Standard excludes prompts/completions from training; 1.3/1.2 Contributor variants permit training for lower prices. These are hosted API models, not weights installed in Larpus.
