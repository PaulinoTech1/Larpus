Red Hat Enterprise Linux 9.6 release notes specify kernel 5.14.0-570.12.1. Its eBPF facility is rebased to upstream Linux 6.12, including BPF tokens and BPF arena support. The eBPF rebase does not make the entire distribution kernel Linux 6.12. Enterprise distributions backport selected changes; compare the vendor kernel build, errata and supported interfaces, not just its upstream base number. This is a reviewed release-note brief, not the complete manual.
