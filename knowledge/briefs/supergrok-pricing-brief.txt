Reviewed 2026-10-09. The official Grok pricing page lists Free USD 0/month, SuperGrok USD 30/month and SuperGrok Plus USD 100/month. SuperGrok includes Grok 4.6, Grok Bot access, connectors, higher feature rate limits, Expert, and image/video generation. Plus adds higher usage, 1080p videos, faster replies and priority access. A subscription price is not a per-token API rate, unlimited usage or proof of an owner's entitlement. Regional checkout, taxes and billing channel may differ; these are dated published prices. No Grok cloud service is connected to Larpus.
