Reviewed 2026-10-09. Grok Bot and Meta Muse are both agents: they can use apps and browsers, retain context and continue multi-step work in cloud computers. Muse emphasises personal goals, everyday tasks, proactive help, a dedicated Secure VM and Sentinel-approved outbound actions; it offers free usage with paid upgrades and a conditional reported referral bonus. Grok Bot emphasises parallel teammates, collaboration, taught routines and scheduled work; Bots for one user share a persistent cloud computer, files and logins. Grok Bot is included in eligible Cursor/SuperGrok/Teams plans with weekly usage and additional token charges. Neither is simply a model or a local offline service.
