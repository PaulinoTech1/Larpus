AMD Ryzen 9 5900X is a Zen 3 desktop processor, codename Vermeer, for socket AM4. AMD lists 12 cores and 24 threads, a 3.7 GHz base clock and boost up to 4.8 GHz. L2 cache is 6 MB and L3 cache 64 MB. Default TDP is 105 W. Memory support is DDR4 up to 3200 MT/s; connectivity is PCIe 4.0. It requires a discrete graphics card. Product limits do not guarantee sustained clocks, whole-system power consumption or operating-system compatibility. This reviewed product brief does not identify the hardware installed in Larpus.
