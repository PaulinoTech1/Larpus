Reviewed 2026-10-09. Meta distinguishes model families: Muse Spark for reasoning/coding and text output; Muse Image (muse-image-1.0) for image generation/editing; Muse Voice Transcribe (muse-voice-transcribe-1.0) for speech-to-text, not TTS; SAM 3.1 (sam-3.1) for image/video segmentation; Muse Glimmer is open-weight and self-hosted, unlike the four hosted families. Muse personal agent is a product, not another Spark model version. Listing Glimmer as self-hostable does not establish compatibility or performance on this station, and no Meta model replaces Larpus Qwen or CPU voice.
