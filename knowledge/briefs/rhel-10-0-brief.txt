Red Hat Enterprise Linux 10.0 release notes specify kernel 6.12.0. Its AMD/Intel 64-bit architecture baseline is x86-64-v3. Listed architectures also include 64-bit ARM, IBM POWER10 or later, and IBM Z. Verify the actual processor feature set, firmware, virtualization exposure and vendor certification before enterprise deployment. The release notes distinguish supported features, Technology Previews, deprecations and known issues. This is a reviewed release-note brief, not the complete manual.
