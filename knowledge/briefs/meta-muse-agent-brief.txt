Reviewed 2026-10-09. Meta Muse is a personal AI agent powered by Muse Spark models. Muse handles goals and multi-step tasks in connected apps and a browser, continues in the background, remembers preferences and asks for approval for sensitive actions. Meta describes a dedicated cloud Secure VM and a separate Sentinel to approve outbound activity. The launch says Muse is free for most needs, with paid plans for heavier use; free does not mean unlimited. The launch describes US rollout on iOS, Android and web. These vendor claims are dated, not independently audited. Muse is an agent product; Muse Spark is its model family. Larpus stores references only and cannot connect accounts or execute Muse tasks.
