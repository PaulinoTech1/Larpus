Reviewed 2026-10-09. BitLocker full management is supported on specified Windows Pro, Enterprise and Education editions; device encryption is also available on eligible Windows Home hardware. Do not equate Home device encryption with enterprise BitLocker policy management or assume Windows Home supports an enterprise Intune enrollment scenario. Confirm the edition and hardware requirements before designing deployment. Verify authorized recovery access and escrow before rollout or boot/firmware changes. Never paste recovery keys into chat.
